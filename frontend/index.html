Choosing an engineering college should never feel like navigating a maze of scattered tabs, broker advice, and unverified promises.

Every year across Andhra Pradesh and India, lakhs of students and their families invest their hard-earned life savings, sacrifices, and dreams into an engineering degree. Yet when counseling begins, they are left to navigate fragmented data, opaque recommendations, and conflicting advice from commercial brokers—without transparent access to real category-wise cutoffs, hidden fee structures, or verified ground-level placement statistics.

We were those exact students. Sitting in front of the APSCHE portal late into the night, agonizing over web option ranks, we experienced firsthand the confusion, stress, and anxiety of making life-defining decisions in the dark. We saw peers misled into paying heavy donations for colleges based on sponsored advertisements rather than actual placement track records.

We built The Bridge ✴ to cut through the noise and deliver the honest, transparent, 100% student-first counseling guide we wished we had when making our own choices.

Quick Guide for MPC Students & Parents

1. How APSCHE Web Options Priority Order Works During AP EAPCET counseling, the computer seat allocation algorithm checks your choices strictly from Option #1 downward. Always place your dream colleges (like AUCE, JNTUK, GVPCE, VRSEC) at the top, followed by target and safe backup options.
2. AP EAPCET & Jagananna Vidya Deevena (JVD) Andhra Pradesh government provides 100% full tuition fee reimbursement (JVD) for eligible students (family income below ₹2.5 Lakh/year) admitted through the Convener Quota (AP EAPCET counseling) in state universities and private unaided engineering colleges.
3. Payment / Deemed Universities (Separate Exams & Discounts) Institutions like VIT-AP, SRM-AP, KL University, GITAM, Amrita, and VFSTR conduct their own entrance exams (VITEEE, SRMJEEE, KLEEE, GAT) and also offer 25% to 100% tuition fee waivers for students with 85%–98%+ Intermediate MPC marks or top EAPCET/JEE ranks.
4. Honest 2025/2026 Placement Metrics & Flags Unlike inflated marketing brochures, this app shows verified 2025 median salaries and flags colleges with sparse campus recruitment drives (<48% placed) so you can make an informed choice.

Key Points to Mention:

  • Comparator: Located in java.util, defines custom sorting with compare(). Multiple sorting sequences possible.
